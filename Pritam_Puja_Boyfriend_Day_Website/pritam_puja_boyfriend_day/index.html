Chapter I

It started with a dance.

We met through a culture fest, as dance partners. Somewhere between the practice hours and all those little moments, something started to feel different.

I already had a little crush on you from NSS. And somehow, you had started liking me from our history class.

There was just something between us during those dance practices that I couldn't shake off.

And then came the white bouquet — your “friendship flower.” 🤍

The very next day, I finally asked you: “Do you like me?” I told you that you didn't have to answer immediately, that you could take your time.

But you confessed. And I confessed too.

And somehow, that little beginning became us.

Two years later, we're still here.
